Share a card with another LoyaltyHive account. Open a card, tap "Share card", and enter their email. They get an email with a link that opens the app, where they can add the card to their wallet. Cards shared with you show who shared them, and you cannot edit or delete them. If you open a share link before you log in or verify your email, the app opens the invite again after that.
